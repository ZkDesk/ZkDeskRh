Business finance, on your terms

ZKdesk brings credit, treasury operations, and payments into one confidential ledger. It is designed for businesses and funds that need to move capital without publishing every position, payment, or counterparty.

Use supported tokenized assets as collateral, assign precise treasury permissions, and set bounded payment mandates. Zero-knowledge proofs and selective disclosure let counterparties verify what they need, while the rest of your financial record stays private.

*Designed for businesses and funds, with confidentiality at the core.
